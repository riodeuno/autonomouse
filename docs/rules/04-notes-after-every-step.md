# Rule 4. Write your notes after every step, not at the end

**Reader:** the worker.

## What the reader does

Write your notes after every step, not at the end: last step done, next step, phase,
iteration. The next session resumes from these.

## Why

A session can end at any step, and the next session knows only what the card says. Notes
written after every step let a fresh session continue from where the work really stands.

## The failure it prevents

A card whose notes name a step already done, so the next session repeats it or trusts a
state that no longer holds.
